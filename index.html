About

I am an Assistant Professor in Aerospace Design and Optimization at the University of Southampton. I completed my PhD in aerospace structures at the Rolls-Royce Composite UTC, University of Bristol (2016), then spent time as a Research Associate in the Vibration UTC at Imperial College London (2017–2020) and as a Lecturer in Aerospace Structures at the University of Strathclyde (2020–2023). Before moving into academia, I worked as a research engineer in robust aircraft design at Airbus UK (2015–2017).

My research develops robust computational methods for the design and optimisation of complex, large-scale nonlinear aerospace systems — spanning nonlinear structural dynamics and friction-damped turbomachinery, dynamic control through novel functional materials and interfaces, and Bayesian/AI-assisted approaches to robust optimisation and identification. I hold a Research Fellowship from the Royal Academy of Engineering / Leverhulme Trust, and am a Fellow of the Royal Aeronautical Society, a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy, and a Chartered Engineer.

Research

AREA·01

Computational Methods for Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

Reduced-order models and computational methods for large-scale nonlinear dynamical systems — including friction-damped turbomachinery, mistuned bladed discs, and jointed structures with contact interfaces.

AREA·02

Dynamic Control via Functional Materials & Interfaces

Dynamic control using novel functional materials and interfaces, including friction interface design, cellular/Kirigami structures, and aeroelastic tailoring of composite wings.

AREA·03

Robust Optimisation & Identification

Non-deterministic approaches — Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, and AI-assisted methods — for robust optimisation and identification of complex aerospace systems.
